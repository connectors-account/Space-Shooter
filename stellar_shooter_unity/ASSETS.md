### Asset provenance

All game artwork was generated specifically for this project; no third-party game sprites or stock asset packs are included.

- `SourceArt/sprite-sheet.png`: original AI-generated artwork, GPT Image 2.5. Source output: https://cdn.abacus.ai/images/748916ca-0696-467e-8d20-1fd8116cb982.png
- `Assets/StellarShooter/Art/player.png`, `scout.png`, `gunner.png`, `bomber.png`, `player-shot.png`, `enemy-shot.png`, `repair.png`, `spread.png`, `shield.png`: transparent, centered crops of that generated sheet, prepared by `Tools/prepare_assets.py`.
- `Assets/StellarShooter/Art/space.png`: original AI-generated background, Nano Banana 2. Source output: https://cdn.abacus.ai/images/daf22b2f-9bc2-41bf-bdac-e746fa7c4ae2.png
- `Assets/StellarShooter/Art/stars.png`: bright-star alpha extraction from the same generated background, for the faster parallax layer.
- `Assets/StellarShooter/Audio/*.wav`: eight original procedural sound effects generated by `Tools/prepare_assets.py`, mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM. No sampled commercial recordings.
- UI font: Unity's built-in `LegacyRuntime.ttf`, referenced by the Editor builder, not redistributed as a separate font.

The downloaded artwork and audio are bundled locally. Opening or running the game does not require the image URLs, generation tools, Python, an AI service, or an internet connection. Internet is normally required for initial Unity installation, licensing, and package resolution. Generated artwork is not asserted to be CC0 or exclusive; its use remains subject to the applicable generation-service terms. Unity and its packages remain under their own licenses.
